Apply for Google Play production access

Frequently asked questions.

Here's some information to help you choose your package.

Why does Google Play require a closed test?

Certain personal developer accounts must meet Google's closed-testing criteria before they can apply for production access. Check your own Play Console for the applicable requirements and current progress.

Does the service include 12 testers for 14 days?

Yes. Both website packages are designed around 12 testers and a 14-day testing period. Your app and closed test must also meet the applicable Google Play requirements.

Is a testing report included?

The ₹799 Testing + Report package includes a testing report. The ₹499 Testing Support package does not include a formal testing report.

Will I definitely receive production access?

No third-party testing provider can guarantee Google's decision. We provide testing support and guidance, while Google Play determines eligibility and production access.

How can I contact your team?

You can call us, start a WhatsApp conversation, or contact us on Telegram using the details in the contact section.

How do I place an order?
